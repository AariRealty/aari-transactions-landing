The Florida Pre-Close Compliance Checklist.

15 items every Florida agent should verify before clearing closing. Built from active files. Use it on your next one.

By Marlenyi Paredes · Florida Real Estate Broker · Aari Transactions

01 Contract mechanics 5 items
  • Effective Date is locked, dated, and distributed to all parties in writing.The date the last party to sign actually delivered the contract. Not the date someone signed.
  • Both buyer and seller signatures confirmed, with delivery timestamps on file.Pull the dotloop or DocuSign audit log. The "delivered" stamp is the controlling event.
  • Inspection Period closure documented and acknowledged in writing.If AS-IS, buyer's election to proceed. If Standard, repair negotiation outcome.
  • Loan Approval Period status verified with the lender in writing."Approved" verbally is not approved. Get the conditional approval letter or CTC.
  • Any extensions, addenda, or counter-offers fully signed and delivered.Unsigned addenda do not modify the contract. Get every page initialed and circulated.
02 Title & escrow 3 items
  • Title commitment received and reviewed for exceptions or clouds.Read schedule B-II. Anything unfamiliar gets a call to title before closing day.
  • HOA / condo estoppel ordered with enough runway for the 10-business-day rule.Florida statute gives the association 10 business days. Order early or get burned.
  • Survey received, reviewed for encroachments, easements, and setback issues.Older Lehigh, Cape Coral, and Fort Myers neighborhoods are full of fence creep.
03 Loan & funding 3 items
  • Clear-to-Close issued by lender in writing, with all conditions cleared.Verbal CTC is not CTC. Get the document.
  • Cash-to-close confirmed with buyer at least 48 hours before closing.Don't let cash-to-close be the closing-day surprise. Have buyer review the CD early.
  • Wire instructions verified by buyer with the title company via call-back to a known number.Wire fraud in Florida real estate is rampant. Buyers verify by phone, not email.
04 Property & insurance 2 items
  • Homeowner's insurance binder is in hand at the title company.Post-2022 Florida market: no binder, no funding. Confirm by Day 5 of the file.
  • All repairs, credits, and concessions documented in writing and reconciled with the CD.What's on the contract should match what's on the closing disclosure. Cross-check.
05 Closing day prep 2 items
  • Final walkthrough scheduled and completed within 24 hours of closing.Walk it. Photograph it. Memorialize any issues in writing before signing.
  • All parties confirmed for closing time, location, and method (in-person or remote).Confirm 24 hours out. Reconfirm the morning of. Power of attorney pre-approved if needed.

How to use this checklist

Run it as the file approaches closing — ideally 72 hours out. Missing items get escalated by phone, not email. A clean run through all 15 is the difference between a smooth Florida closing and a deal that slips by three days.
