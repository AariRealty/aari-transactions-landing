Common questions

Questions agents ask before their first file.

What does it cost?
Coordination fees start at $349 (one side) and $599 (both sides). Standalone services like Offer Prep run from $95–$375. Full pricing is on the pricing section above — every fee disclosed, no surprises.
When do I pay?
TC services (coordination) are charged at closing. Standalone services and memberships are paid upfront. We never invoice you before the work starts.
What if my deal doesn't close?
You don't pay the coordination fee. Upfront services like Offer Prep are kept since the work was done. We share the risk on the close, not the prep.
How fast do you start working?
Same day. Files submitted by 2pm are touched the same business day. No 48-hour intake delay. Title and lender hear from us before you log off.
Why does broker-owned matter?
Most TCs are unlicensed contractors. Aari is owned and operated by a Florida real estate broker — every file gets reviewed for compliance before close. RESPA, addenda, escrow trail, missing disclosures. Caught 48 hours before closing, not at the table.
Do you handle both sides?
Yes. Dual representation coordination is a dedicated service ($599). Separate file tabs per side, dual disclosures, joint scheduling.
How does this work with my brokerage?
You stay agent of record under your brokerage. We coordinate as an outside vendor. Your broker's compliance file stays clean — we deliver a complete audit folder to your brokerage drive at close.
Will my client interact with you?
Only when needed. We communicate primarily with you, title, and lender. Client contact is your decision per file — some agents want us to call, some keep it agent-only. RESPA-clean either way.
