Common questions

Quick answers.

About the service
Do you only work with one brokerage?
No. Aari Transactions is brokerage independent. Agents from any brokerage are welcome and get the same standard, the same TC team, and the same compliance review.
When do I pay?
TC fees are billed at closing through your title settlement DA. CDA (Commission Disbursement Authorization) is required at intake, per Service Agreement Section 2(a). Listing Coordinator and add-ons are paid upfront via Stripe at the time of order. Memberships are recurring.
What happens if a deal falls through?
Pay only when you close. If the file falls through, for any reason, you owe nothing on TC. We don't bill for work that didn't get you paid. Upfront services (Listing Coordinator, add-ons) are non-refundable once work commences.
How fast do you assign a TC?
Same-day receipt confirmation. TC assigned the same business day. Every file.
Are you Florida only?
Yes. Aari services Florida files exclusively. Every TC is Florida-trained, fluent in FAR/BAR and Florida REALTORS® forms, and we maintain Florida-specific compliance standards. We do not take on out-of-state files.
What does compliance review cover?
Documents uploaded, organized, and audited against your brokerage's compliance standard before close. Coordination with your broker's compliance department. CDA requested.
Are there any late fees?
Yes. A flat $25 late fee applies to any unpaid invoice past due, across memberships, add-ons, and transaction coordination fees. We do not stack daily charges. The late fee is one-time per overdue invoice.
About memberships
Does membership lock me into using Aari for every deal?
No. Membership exclusivity only applies to files where Aari has performed pre-contract work at your direction (offer preparation, listing preparation, MLS setup, or pre-execution coordination). Files you write yourself, walk-in offers, lot deals, brokerage-mandated handling, and confidentiality cases are entirely yours to manage. We call this the "Aari-Engaged File" rule. Full details in your Service Agreement.
Is membership worth it?
Yes if you close 3+ TC files per month. Each membership pays for itself through the TC discount alone (Starter $30 off, Producer $50 off per file). The monthly credits are additional value on top.
What can I use membership credits for?
Membership credits apply to three add-on services: Offer Prep · Basic ($69), Listing Docs ($99), and File Organization ($99). Credits do NOT apply to Offer Prep · Complete or MLS Setup. Those services are billed separately at member rates.
What other benefits come with Producer membership?
Producer members get top TC priority on every file submission. Plus an Activity Bonus: 2 active TC files in a month unlocks 4 extra credits that month.
Can I cancel my membership?
Yes. Cancel anytime. No long-term contract. You keep access to your benefits through the end of the billing period.
Is there an annual plan?
Yes. Starter annual is $789 (saves 17%). Producer annual is $1,289 (saves 17%). Subscribe Starter Annual · Subscribe Producer Annual.
