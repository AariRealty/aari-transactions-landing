      Common questions

      Questions agents ask before their first file.

      What does it cost?
      Here's what you pay. $399 for one side. $599 for both. Standalone work starts at $69. The pricing on this page is the whole pricing. We don't sneak in admin fees, review fees, or any of the line items brokerages quietly tack on at the closing table.
      When do I pay?
      Depends on what you're paying for. Coordination fees come out at closing, on the DA, after the deal funds. Standalone services and memberships are paid upfront before we start the work. You never get an invoice from us before you submit. Ever.
      What if my deal doesn't close?
      Then you don't pay the coordination fee. Period. Standalone work you paid upfront (Offer Prep, Listing Docs) stays paid because the work happened, but everything else is a "no close, no fee" deal. We carry the risk on the close. You carry the risk on the prep. That's the trade.
      How fast do you start working?
      Same day. Files submitted before 2pm get touched the same business day. After 2pm, you're working with us by 10am the next morning. No 48 hour intake delay, no "we'll get to it Monday." Title and lender hear from us before you log off for the night.
      Why does broker-owned matter?
      Because most TCs aren't licensed. They're unlicensed contractors hoping nothing on the file blows up. Aari is owned and run by a Florida real estate broker, which means every file gets a compliance review before close. RESPA. Addenda. Escrow trail. Missing disclosures. We catch the gaps 48 hours before closing, not at the table when there's no time to fix anything.
      Do you handle both sides?
      Yes. Both sides is its own service, billed at $599 instead of $399. You get separate file tabs for each side, separate disclosures, and joint scheduling so neither side waits on the other. Same broker review on both.
      How does this work with my brokerage?
      Easy. You stay agent of record under your brokerage. We work as an outside vendor on your file. Your broker's compliance folder stays clean because we deliver a complete audit folder to your brokerage drive the day the deal closes. Your broker never has to chase you for missing docs.
      Will my client interact with you?
      Only if you want them to. We communicate primarily with you, title, and lender. Whether your client hears from us is your call per file. Some agents want us looped in on calls. Some keep it strictly agent only. Either way, RESPA clean.
