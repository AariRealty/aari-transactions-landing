Common questions

Quick answers.

Do you only work with one brokerage?
No. Aari Transactions is brokerage independent. Agents from any brokerage are welcome and get the same standard, the same TC team, and the same compliance review.
When do I pay?
TC fees are billed at closing through your title settlement DA. CDA (Commission Disbursement Authorization) is required at intake, per Service Agreement Section 2(a). Listing Coordinator and add-ons are paid upfront via Stripe at the time of order. Memberships are recurring.
What happens if a deal falls through?
TC fees are billed only at closing. If the file doesn't close (through no fault of the agent), you don't pay. Upfront services (Listing Coordinator, add-ons) are non-refundable once work commences.
How fast do you assign a TC?
Same-day receipt confirmation. TC assigned within 24 business hours. You'll know who is running your file before the next business day ends.
Are you Florida only?
Florida is our specialty and primary market. We may take on out-of-state work selectively. Ask if you have a non-Florida file.
What does compliance review cover?
Documents uploaded, organized, and audited against your brokerage's compliance standard before close. Coordination with your broker's compliance department. CDA requested.
Is membership worth it?
If you close 2+ deals per month and write offers regularly, yes. Both tiers include flexible monthly credits (use them on Offer Prep, Listing Docs, or MLS Setup), TM discounts, and priority TC assignment. Producer also unlocks an activity bonus when you have 2 files actively under TC management. Membership only requires Aari to coordinate "Aari-Engaged Files" (files where Aari prepared the offer, listing, or pre-contract docs). Walk-ins and files you prefer to handle directly are not subject to that requirement.
Are there any late fees?
Yes. A flat $25 late fee applies to any unpaid invoice past due — across memberships, add-ons, and transaction coordination fees. We do not stack daily charges. The late fee is one-time per overdue invoice.
