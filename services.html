Billing & fee terms

TC files (One Side, Both Sides) are billed only at close. If the deal does not close through no fault of the agent, no coordination fee is owed. Upfront services and memberships are non-refundable once work begins. Membership exclusivity: files Aari prepares pre-contract at your direction (offer prep, listing prep, MLS setup, or pre-execution coordination) stay with Aari for transaction coordination through close. Walk-in offers, lot deals, brokerage-mandated handling, and confidentiality cases are exempt. Full definitions in the Service Agreement and Terms of Service. Prices subject to change.
