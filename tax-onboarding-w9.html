Onboarding step

Your tax form (W-9)

One quick step so we can pay you correctly. As a referral associate you are an independent contractor, so we need a W-9 on file before we send any referral payouts.

Why we ask. A W-9 gives us your legal name and taxpayer ID so we can report your referral income to the IRS and send you a Form 1099-NEC each year. We collect it once, and it stays on file. Without it on file, payouts may be delayed and backup withholding could apply.

1

Open the fillable W-9

Use the official IRS form. It is free, and it is a fillable PDF, so you type right into the boxes on your computer or phone. No printing or handwriting needed.

Open fillable IRS Form W-9 →
2

Type into it and save

Type into the boxes: your legal name, tax classification (most associates pick "Individual/sole proprietor"), address, and taxpayer ID (your SSN, or EIN if you use one). Then sign and date.

Important: press Save or Download to keep your entries, do not just close the tab. On a phone, open the form in a files or PDF app first so you can type and save.

3

Upload it in the portal

Upload your saved W-9 through the secure box below. Please do not email it, since it contains your taxpayer ID.

How we protect this. Your W-9 is stored in encrypted, access controlled storage and is never collected by email or shown on this page. Only the broker and authorized staff can access it, and it is retained as required by law.

Aari Referrals LLC will issue Form 1099-NEC by January 31 each year to associates paid $600 or more in referral compensation during the prior calendar year. This page is general information, not tax advice. For questions about your tax classification, consult a tax professional.
