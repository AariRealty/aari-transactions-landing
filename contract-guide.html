How Days Are Counted in Florida Real Estate Contracts
Day 0 Rule
The Effective Date is Day 0 and is not counted. Counting begins the day after. Example: Effective Date August 14, a 10-day inspection period ends August 24.
Saturday Counts Mid-Period
All days count when moving through a period — including Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays. A Saturday in the middle of a 10-day period still counts as one of those 10 days.
Weekend / Holiday at Deadline
If the deadline day itself lands on a Saturday, Sunday, or national legal holiday, it automatically extends to the next business day. Example: Day 10 lands on Saturday, deadline moves to Monday.
Time of Deadline
Deadlines expire at 11:59 PM on the deadline day. Standard F of the FR/BAR contract confirms all periods use calendar days.
Time is of the Essence
All deadlines in the FR/BAR contract are time of the essence. Missing even one can result in automatic termination or loss of deposit.
Initial Deposit
The Initial Deposit deadline uses calendar days — the same as every other period in the FR/BAR contract. Standard F contains no business days exception. If the deadline lands on a weekend or holiday, it extends to the next business day. Always verify the exact period against your executed contract.
Day 0 Rule
The Effective Date is Day 0 — defined as the last dated initials or signature by either party. Counting begins the day after.
Saturday Counts Mid-Period
All days count including Saturdays and Sundays when moving through a period. A Saturday mid-period still counts as one of the days.
Weekend / Holiday at Deadline
If the deadline day itself lands on a Saturday, Sunday, or national legal holiday, it extends to the next business day. Standard R of NABOR confirms this rule.
Time of Deadline
All deadlines except the Closing Date expire at 11:59 PM Eastern Time. The NABOR contract explicitly states this.
Time is of the Essence
Only the Closing Date is time of the essence under NABOR. Other deadlines carry consequences but a minor delay does not automatically give the other party the right to terminate.
Initial Deposit
Always verify the deposit period and day-counting method against your executed NABOR contract. If the deadline lands on a weekend or holiday, it extends to the next business day per Standard R. If Buyer defaults, Seller damages are limited to deposits actually made — not deposits agreed to be made.
