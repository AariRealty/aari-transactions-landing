Introduce a fellow agent

Know an agent who'd actually use a broker-owned TC?

If you've had a clean experience with Aari and there's an agent in your circle who would benefit from the same thing, this is the way to make the introduction. No pitch on our end. We reach out personally.

  1. You fill out the form. Your name and email so we know who to thank. Their name and email so we know who to introduce ourselves to.
  2. We send them a short note. From Marlenyi, not from a marketing list. Tells them you thought of them, explains who we are in two sentences, leaves the door open.
  3. That's it. Whether they reach out is up to them. Whether they ever submit a file is up to them. No pressure ever runs back to your name.

A note on how this works

Aari Transactions does not pay referral fees for introductions. RESPA Section 8 prohibits payment for referrals of settlement services and we take that seriously. This form is purely a way to make a warm introduction between people. If your introduction leads to a meaningful connection, expect a handwritten thank-you note from Marlenyi.
