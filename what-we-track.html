02

Compliance most TCs miss.

Post-NAR-settlement compensation rider, Florida-specific coastal disclosures, federal filings, and CFPB math — all tracked as first-class tasks, not afterthoughts.

Federal Filing
FIRPTA withholding
Foreign seller status flagged from the contract. Withholding + affidavit tracked before closing so the buyer doesn't inherit the tax hit.
Federal Filing
FinCEN cash report
Entity + cash purchase auto-flagged. FinCEN reporting requirement surfaced at CTC · no last-minute scramble.
Federal Disclosure
Lead-Based Paint pre-1978
Property year checked · disclosure task auto-inserted when required. HUD-compliant form language.
Florida Coastal
CCCL affidavit
Coastal Construction Control Line disclosure attached for oceanfront + waterfront parcels · a Florida-only requirement most TCs skip.
Post-NAR Settlement
Comp Agreement + Rider GG
Confirms both brokers signed. On file before proceed-to-CTC · protects the file when the buyer's compensation is challenged.
CFPB Rule
3-day CD window
Closing Disclosure receipt date logged · 3 business-day window auto-calculated. Green/red banner on the file when the closing date is safe or too soon.
TRID
TRID business-day math
TRID counts Saturdays as business days · Standard F does not. Both rules coded separately, applied to the right deadline.
Florida Rule
Weekend + federal-holiday roll
Every deadline rolls forward on a Florida weekend or federal holiday. Generated for any year automatically · no hand-maintained tables.
Deadline Engine
Paragraph-cited dates
Every deadline row cites the exact paragraph ("Para 2(a)", "9(c)", "12(b)(iv)"). Manual overrides preserve the paragraph note.
03

Safety we enforce.

The guardrails that catch mistakes before they cost the deal — every one runs in code, on every file, without a human remembering to check.

Guard
Unsigned SA + unpaid = absolute lock
A file cannot advance to Under Contract with an unsigned Service Agreement or unpaid invoice. Not bypassable · legal + billing integrity.
Guard
Closing-date mismatch banner
If the contract closing date and the file's closing date drift, the drawer flashes a banner naming both dates + which doc holds the truth.
Guard
Duplicate file auto-detection
Same address + similar terms opens a duplicate banner naming the twin · prevents double-billing the client and double-paying the TC.
Communication Rule
Thread 3 · never the buyer
Escrow thread never CCs the buyer. Coded into the email engine · impossible to fat-finger.
Audit
Move-anyway logged for FREC
Every early stage move is written to an append-only audit log with who + when + from-stage + to-stage · reconstructable on demand.
Honesty Guard
No contract type = no dates
If we can't confirm which form, we show nothing rather than defaulting to FR/BAR and being wrong on NABOR or vacant land.
