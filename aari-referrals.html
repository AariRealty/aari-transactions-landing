Florida referral basics

What is a referral-only brokerage in Florida?

Simple: you place your active Florida license with a brokerage, refer the people you already know, and get paid when they close. No listings, no showings, no MLS dues. It's fully legal in Florida as long as your license stays active.

Referral-only means exactly that. Stay inside the lines and your license stays protected.

You can

  • Refer friends, family, neighbors, coworkers, and past clients
  • Refer your own personal, professional, and social-media contacts
  • Refer your own transactions for a referral fee
  • Earn referral income while keeping your license active

You can't

  • List homes, represent buyers or sellers, or write contracts
  • Show homes, hold open houses, or use MLS / lockbox access
  • Submit cold referrals or internet leads from people who don't know you
  • Hold your license at another brokerage at the same time

When do I get paid? Within 5 business days after the company receives and keeps the referral fee (30-day safe harbor) and your documentation is complete, in Good Standing.

Are there transaction fees on my payout? No. There are no transaction fees on referral payouts. Only the membership fees apply.

What if my referral goes to Aari Realty? You earn your 80% share only if a broker-to-broker referral fee is actually paid to Aari Referrals. You earn no portion of the transaction commission itself.

Just got licensed, can I join? Yes. Newly licensed Florida agents are welcome, as long as you meet licensing and Good Standing requirements.

Do I need MLS or Realtor membership? No. Referral associates are referral-only and don't need MLS access or REALTOR® membership.

Can I go back to active sales later? Yes. Contact the broker about transferring to Aari Realty LLC. A $50 reactivation fee applies.
