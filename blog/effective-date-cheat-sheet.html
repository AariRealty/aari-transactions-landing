Effective Date, at a glance

By Marlenyi Paredes · Founder of Aari Transactions

The Effective Date is the day most deadlines on your file count from. Each Florida form defines it in its own words, and that definition decides which day counts. Check your form for how your contract defines it.

What the Effective Date controls The deadlines that count from it

Plain English: deposit deadlines, the Inspection Period, the Loan Approval Period on financed deals, title and HOA document deadlines, and sometimes the Closing Date can all count from the Effective Date, as your contract states. One wrong day here moves every one of them.

Where to find the definition The Effective Date paragraph of your contract

Plain English: find the paragraph in your form that defines Effective Date and read it word for word. Ask two questions: does it turn on the last signature or initial, and does it also require delivery to the other side? Forms do not all answer those the same way. Check your form for how your contract defines it.

Lock it on the file One date, one place

Plain English: once you confirm the date against the executed contract, write it in one place on the file and count every deadline from that one field. When the definition or the facts are unclear, ask your broker before you calendar it.
