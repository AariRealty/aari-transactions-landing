Your email categories

Three categories. Each can be turned off independently. Transactional cannot be disabled per CAN-SPAM.

Transactional emails

Intake confirmations. Coordinator assignments. File status updates. Membership changes. These are tied to a specific file or account event you initiated.

Cannot be disabled per CAN-SPAM transactional carve-out.

Post-close review requests

A one-time email to your clients 24 hours after closing asking for a review. Sent to your clients (not you). You control whether this fires for your files.

Marketing and newsletters

Win-back check-ins if you go quiet. Monthly broker note. Occasional broadcasts about new services or policy changes.

How this works

One-click unsubscribe takes effect immediately on save. Transactional emails (intake confirmation, file status, membership changes) continue under CAN-SPAM's transactional message carve-out. Marketing and review-request opt-outs are honored for all future sends. If you want full account deletion instead of opt-out, email hello@aaritransactions.com.
